## Detailed notes

### science_en_056 ⚠️

- **Question**: Which fundamental particle is responsible for giving other particles mass?
- **Marked correct**: The Higgs boson
- **Clarity concern**: Strictly speaking the Higgs FIELD imparts mass; the Higgs boson is the field's quantized excitation. The fun_fact correctly states "The Higgs field permeates all space" — so it self-resolves. This is a standard educational simplification used by CERN's own outreach material.
- **Suggested fix**: None required. The fun_fact already provides the rigorous framing; the question-level shorthand is acceptable.

### science_en_064 ⚠️

- **Question**: What state of matter is a candle flame?
- **Marked correct**: Mostly gas, plus tiny incandescent particles
- **Distractor / clarity concern**: "Plasma" is given as a distractor — but a large fraction of science educators (and some Wikipedia treatments) call candle flames "weakly ionized plasma". Rigorous physics agrees ordinary diffusion flames lack the ionization fraction (typically <0.01%) to qualify as true plasma. The marked answer aligns with the rigorous view, and the fun_fact directly addresses the controversy ("Most candle flames aren't hot enough to ionise air into plasma").
- **Suggested fix**: None required. The fun_fact resolves the ambiguity, and the rigorous-physics framing is defensible. Optional tightening: rephrase question to "What state of matter is an ordinary candle flame, strictly speaking?" — but not necessary.

### science_en_089 ⚠️

- **Question**: Why don't your eyes get tired even though they move constantly all day?
- **Marked correct**: Eye muscles are the fastest and most efficient in the body
- **Factual concern**: The "fastest" claim is defensible (extraocular saccades reach ~900°/sec, very high angular velocities). "Most efficient" is loosely defined — there's no single standard metric. The fun_fact backs the claim with the more rigorous mitochondrial-density / fast-twitch-fibre observation.
- **Suggested fix**: None required. The fun_fact provides the rigorous mechanism. If tightening is desired: change "most efficient" → "have very high mitochondrial density" in the answer text — but not necessary; the simplified phrasing reads well in-game.
