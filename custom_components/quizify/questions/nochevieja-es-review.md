## Summary

- New questions: 40 (easy 17, medium 18, hard 5); pack mix after the update: 51 easy / 63 medium / 26 hard. Pack size: 140.
- Every 2026 fact comes from a web source read on 2026-10-19 (listed per question); at least two independent sources for the result of every event, except where noted below. The cut-off is mid-October 2026, the latest event is the October 2026 Nobel Prizes. Every question names the month it refers to.
- Chosen for durability: winners, results and records. Details that sources disagreed on (exact scores of tennis finals, the age of a cyclist, the Champions League shootout tally, medal totals) are deliberately not asked.
- Duplicate check: compared with the existing questions of the pack and its two sibling packs; no overlap (the existing questions are about traditions, the new block is about 2026).
- Plus 10 questions on the Reyes Magos / Epiphany (6 January), the main gift day in Spain, which falls outside the Christmas window. These are timeless, so their sources are reference articles rather than 2026 reports.
- No picture questions and no estimate questions (estimate-parity test untouched).
- Single-source items: Bulgaria joining the euro on 1 January 2026 (Wikipedia 2026 article) and the ski-mountaineering Olympic debut (Wikipedia Olympics article) are widely known planned events and are consistent with the rest of the coverage read.
