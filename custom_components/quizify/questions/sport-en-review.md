### sport_en_001 ⚠️

- **Question**: Which event was contested at the Olympics from 1900 to 1920 but never returned as a regular discipline?
- **Marked correct**: Tug of war
- **Difficulty concern**: Marked easy but recalling that tug of war was an Olympic sport 1900-1920 is not common knowledge — this is medium territory.
- **Suggested fix**: bump difficulty to `medium`.

### sport_en_005 ⚠️

- **Question**: Solo synchronised swimming was an Olympic event in 1984, 1988 and 1992. What's odd about its name?
- **Marked correct**: A single swimmer was 'synchronising' only with the music
- **Fun-fact concern**: "The IOC quietly admitted that 'synchronised' with no partner made little sense" is folklore — the actual reason for dropping it was that the team event was added in Atlanta 1996 and they couldn't have three sub-disciplines.
- **Suggested fix**: revise fun fact to cite the real reason (replaced by team event).

### sport_en_008 ⚠️

- **Question**: Which Olympic event was scored using a system where competitors deliberately tried to *not* hit the target perfectly?
- **Marked correct**: Plunge for distance (diving for distance underwater without moving)
- **Clarity concern**: Plunge for Distance has no "target" — divers floated motionless and the drift distance was measured. The "deliberately tried to *not* hit the target" framing doesn't fit. The question conflates an unfamiliar event with a misleading description.
- **Suggested fix**: rewrite to describe the event accurately ("which Olympic event measured passive drift distance after a dive?").

### sport_en_021 ⚠️

- **Question**: Why are the seams on an American football shaped the way they are?
- **Marked correct**: Originally to make pig-bladder balls easier to grip and sew, later retained for aerodynamics
- **Factual + clarity concern**: "Seams shaped the way they are" is ambiguous — does it mean the laces (closure stitching) or the overall prolate ball shape? Two different design histories. The marked-correct answer mixes both. Better to delete than force a single-answer framing on a question that doesn't have one.
- **Suggested fix**: delete (the question can't be cleanly resolved without rewriting the premise).

### sport_en_022 🚨

- **Marked correct**: Green Bay Packers (correct)
- **Fun-fact fail**: "five share offerings since 1923" — the Packers have actually held six (1923, 1935, 1950, 1997, 2011, 2021).
- **Suggested fix**: update fun fact to say "six share offerings".

### sport_en_024 ⚠️

- **Question**: Which footballer has scored a 'perfect hat-trick' (one with left foot, one with right foot, one header) the most times in his career?
- **Marked correct**: Cristiano Ronaldo
