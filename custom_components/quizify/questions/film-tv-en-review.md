## Detailed notes

### film_en_019 ℹ️

- **Question**: Why was old cellulose nitrate film stock such a threat to film archives?
- **Marked correct**: It could keep burning even under water
- **Distractors**: 'Exploded when exposed to sound' is absurd and easily dismissed.

### film_en_024 ⚠️

- **Question**: What unusual admission rule did Alfred Hitchcock demand for 'Psycho' (1960)?
- **Marked correct**: No one was admitted after the film had started
- **Difficulty**: Labelled easy, but the no-late-admission gimmick is film-buff trivia that most adults would not know; medium fits better.

### film_en_025 ⚠️

- **Question**: How did inventor Richard Hollingshead test his idea of a drive-in cinema in 1933?
- **Marked correct**: He hung bedsheets between trees and ran a projector on his car
- **Factual**: Hollingshead's driveway tests (screen nailed to trees, projector on car hood, radio behind screen) are generally dated to 1932 (patent filed Aug 1932, granted May 1933); only the opening in Camden was June 1933. 'Bedsheets' also varies by source. Checked from memory of standard accounts, not web.

### film_en_037 ⚠️

- **Difficulty**: Labelled easy, but most adults would not know the shooting location; medium is more realistic.

### film_en_039 ⚠️
