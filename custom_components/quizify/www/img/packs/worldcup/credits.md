## Why this pair is all pre-1955

Football photography after the 1950s is comprehensively rights-managed, and
what is freely licensed on Commons is almost entirely CC BY-SA. Everything that
is genuinely public domain sits in the first four tournaments, which is where
this set had to be built. A modern World Cup picture question would need a
licence this project does not have.

## Edited, and why

* **`maracana-1950.webp`** — the print carried a Spanish caption burned along
  the bottom naming the final, the date **16/7/50** and the Maracanã. The
  question asks what the photograph shows, so the caption was the answer.
  Cropped off.
* **`world-cup-poster-1950.webp`** — an **"ARQUIVO NACIONAL"** stamp sat in the
  top right corner of the scan. Cropped off for the same reason the Potato
  Eaters watermark was rejected in the food set: licence-clear is not
  ship-clear.

## Two questions that lean on knowledge, not recognition

The squad photograph and the trophy could in principle be any team and any cup.
Both questions name what the picture is and ask for the fact around it — which
suits a World Cup pack, where the subject *is* the history. The other three are
straight recognition: a stadium, a poster, a ball in the net.

## Sizes

1100 px on the long edge (the Maracanã print keeps its native 1024 px), WebP at
quality 80, 36–213 KB each, 529 KB for the folder. Each was rendered at 340 px —
the width the card gets on a 390 px phone — and looked at before being kept.
The poster is the one that had to survive it: at card width the lines *IV
CAMPEONATO MUNDIAL DE FUTEBOL* and *BRASIL* are still readable, which is what
its question rests on.
