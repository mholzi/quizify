## Dropped, and why

* **A second file of the Potato Eaters** (`Vincent Van Gogh - The Potato
  Eaters.png`) — licence fine, but the reproduction carries a *"Piazilla"*
  watermark burned into the lower left corner. It is not visible in a
  thumbnail on Commons and is plainly visible on a quiz card. Replaced with the
  7698 px museum-grade scan, which is clean.
* **Köhler's botanical plates** for cacao, vanilla and coffee — all public
  domain, all 410–513 px on the long edge, below the 900–1200 px this pack
  keeps to. Chocolate and coffee are therefore still unrepresented; a larger
  public-domain plate would fix that.

## A note on brightness

Two of the five are dark paintings — the Van Gogh interior and the Dutch
banquet still life. Both were checked at 340 px specifically for that: the
lamp-lit faces and the bowl carry the Van Gogh, and the lobster is bright red
against a dark ground. A third dark subject would have been one too many.

## Sizes

1100 px on the long edge, WebP at quality 80, 40–139 KB each, 362 KB for the
folder. Each was rendered at 340 px — the width the card gets on a 390 px
phone — and looked at before being kept.
